About the data

  • Australia: Department of Home Affairs student visa grant rates, primary applicants outside Australia, Higher Education Sector.
  • New Zealand: Immigration New Zealand annual student visa decisions, offshore applicants.
  • United Kingdom: Home Office entry clearance visa outcomes, Student route main applicants; withdrawn and lapsed applications excluded.

Counts come from the public Country-to-Visa dataset on Zenodo; the Australia and New Zealand tables were prepared with vitalii-kaplan/au-nz-comparison. Approval rate = approved ÷ (approved + refused). Groups differ between countries (course levels, where people apply from), so compare with care.
